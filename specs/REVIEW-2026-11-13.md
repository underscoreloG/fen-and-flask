## Design finding (the important one)
The economy is capped by customers, not by production: after 26 days the bot holds 34-73 tonic, 102-148 salve and 82-114 glowdraught unsold. With 8-10 customers an evening, tables, deep tables, the mortar, the second cauldron, the sickle, the beds and two of the toads would add no coins at all; only the signboard and the daily order raised income. - FIXED by design: tables 5 and 6 and the signboard each bring 2 more customers, a new lantern sign brings 3 (17 at most), prices were raised and reordered (4050 coins for 14 items), and the health test now checks that income rises after the lantern sign and that no potion piles up beyond 30 units.
